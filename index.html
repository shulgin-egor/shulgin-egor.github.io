Bio

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), where I work with Éric Moulines and Eduard Gorbunov. My research focuses on optimization for machine learning, with an emphasis on understanding and improving how large models are trained. I am also interested in distributed and private learning.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), advised by Peter Richtárik. During my PhD, I spent time at Apple and Samsung AI Center (Cambridge, UK), where I worked on heterogeneous, efficient, and personalized federated learning.

Apart from research, I am also passionate about hiking/backpacking, alpine skiing, and rationality.
